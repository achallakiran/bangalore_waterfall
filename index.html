How to play

Drive a lap, cross the chequered line to start the clock, and keep to the road: grass slows you down and potholes throw the wheel. Every road climbs or drops somewhere, so ease off before the crest.

W ↑ gas

S ↓ brake and reverse

A D ← → steer

Space handbrake · C camera · R restart

Esc P pause · F full screen

On a phone the four round buttons steer, brake and go. Two fingers work at once. Drag on the scene to look around, pinch to zoom.

About

Hill Loop Track is built from voxel cubes in the style of the Bangalore street-block gallery. Roads, hills, potholes and traffic are all cubes drawn with three.js.

Everything runs on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
